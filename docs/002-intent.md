# 002. Intent and alignment

Every repository keeps its intent in `docs/`: goals (vision, users, objectives, non-goals, success criteria, priorities, constraints), glossary, assumptions, decisions and specs. The owner writes the goals. Agents may interview, transcribe and format, never invent.

Before any diagnosis an agent states what it understood and waits for the owner to confirm. Tasks state What, Why, Where, Do not touch, Done when and If unsure.

The traffic light decides when to ask: green is decided and recorded, yellow is proposed and waits, red stops. When sources disagree, safety and the task's Do not touch come first, then the task, the goals and specs, the accepted decisions, the closest `AGENTS.md`, the root `AGENTS.md`, the standard and the owner's preferences, and last the agent's general knowledge.
